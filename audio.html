Audio Editing Steps

  1. Record the voice clip: Capture the narration through the computer microphone.
  2. Clean the recording: Remove unwanted silence or noise and trim the track.
  3. Improve playback: Normalize the volume and apply fade-in and fade-out effects.
  4. Export the audio: Render the final recording in MP3 format for browser playback.
